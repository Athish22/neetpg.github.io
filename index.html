Where the data comes from

Every number here is counted from the final seat allotment results that the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) publishes as PDFs on mcc.nic.in after each round of All India counselling. A closing rank is the highest All India rank that was allotted that seat in that round. The figure under it is how many candidates were allotted.

This covers the MCC rounds only: All India quota, deemed universities, central universities, DNB and AFMS seats. State quota counselling, fees, stipends and bond terms are not included, because no official source publishes them in one place.

Past closing ranks are a guide, not a promise. Seat counts, reservation rules and the number of candidates change every year. Check the MCC information bulletin before you fill choices. This site is not affiliated with MCC, NBEMS or any counselling service.
